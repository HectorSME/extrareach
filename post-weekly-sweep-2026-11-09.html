ExtraReach is still in the planning stage. This week’s sweep (roughly November 2–9) is a thin one. The main news is that International Passive House Open Days are a few days away, with new North American listings now live. There’s also a design-tool note for teams on the Passive House Institute path, plus rebate and policy deadlines worth marking on the calendar.

International Passive House Open Days Nov 13 to 15

The Passive House Network (PHN) has posted its North American listings for International Passive House Open Days, November 13–15. Several stops are in New York and New Jersey. There are no Virginia, Maryland or DC listings yet, and PHN says more are coming. Only two of the posted buildings are already certified; the rest are still in design or under construction. It’s the nearest chance this fall to walk real Passive House buildings in the region, and a good source of contemporary case studies.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Put the weekend on the calendar if a New York or New Jersey trip is workable. Watch the PHN listings page for Mid-Atlantic stops that may still be added.

Sources: Passive House Network Open Days

Street Smart Brooklyn certified all-electric fourplex

Street Smart, at 369 Manhattan Ave in Brooklyn, is a four-unit, all-electric infill building certified by the Passive House Institute (PHI). It’s contemporary, and it’s one of the two Open Days stops that are already certified—so visitors can walk a finished Passive House home rather than a construction site.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

A useful contemporary multifamily reference for all-electric Passive House planning. The New York State Energy Research and Development Authority case study is linked below.

Sources: Passive House Network Open Days · NYSERDA Street Smart case study (PDF)

475 HQ certified commercial retrofit

475 HQ, at 369 Warren St in Brooklyn, is a commercial retrofit with final Phius (Passive House Institute US) ZERO REVIVE 2021 certification dated February 10, 2026. REVIVE is Phius’s pilot certification program for retrofits. The same building also meets EnerPHit, PHI’s retrofit standard. It’s contemporary, though not a home—still a walkable certified example during Open Days.

Other Open Days stops are still in progress, in design or under construction: 658 Briggs in Ellenville, New York; the Raritan Valley farmhouse rebuild in Lebanon, New Jersey; ABC No Rio; and VITA NY.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

ExtraReach is new construction, so REVIVE and EnerPHit don’t change our path. For Mid-Atlantic retrofit friends, 475 HQ is a current certified example of both Phius and PHI retrofit tracks in one building.

Sources: Passive House Network Open Days · Phius certified project: 369 Warren Street

designPH 3.0 for Passive House Institute designers

PHN spotlighted designPH 3.0 on November 6. designPH is PHI’s SketchUp-to-Passive House Planning Package (PHPP) design tool. Version 3.0 adds monthly heating and cooling demand estimates and better ground and shading modeling. PHI actually released it on July 28, 2026, so this is a Resources note rather than new news. It pairs with last week’s item on METr (Modeled Energy Transfer), Phius’s cloud modeling tool.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Useful if we work with a designer on the PHI / PHPP path. ExtraReach’s Phius track still points at METr, covered in the November 2 sweep.

Sources: Passive House Network: designPH 3 is here · PHI: new release designPH 3.0

Pepco EmPOWER reservations end Dec 31

Under EmPOWER Maryland, the state’s utility-funded efficiency program, Pepco’s Home Performance with ENERGY STAR rebate reservations are still due December 31. No change to the program itself this week—just a deadline worth locking in if a Pepco-service address is on your list.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Mark December 31 if Pepco service territory is in play for a Home Performance rebate reservation.

Sources: Pepco Home Performance rebates

BGE heat pump water heater rebate ends Dec 31

Baltimore Gas and Electric (BGE) is still running its mail-in heat pump water heater rebate of up to $1,600 through December 31.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Another December 31 deadline for BGE-territory projects choosing a heat pump water heater.

Sources: BGE heat pump water heater rebate

Still watching

In DC, Emergency Act A26-0457 remains in effect through January 27, 2027. Temporary Act A26-0430 is under congressional review; a Council report projects it becomes law on December 19, 2026 (that date is projected, not independently confirmed here). Final net-zero rules are still due by December 31, 2027. The ban on on-site fuel combustion for new buildings over 10,000 sq ft applies to permits filed after December 31, 2026. No permanent bill has been introduced yet. The DC Sustainable Energy Utility (DCSEU) Income Qualified Efficiency Fund early-bird deadline is December 18 (final deadline January 15), as noted in our November 2 sweep.

New York’s all-electric rule remains paused. No U.S. Supreme Court petition shows yet on Climate Case Chart, and the petition window runs to about November 24. Source: Climate Case Chart.

EmPOWER Maryland’s 2027–2029 cycle still has no Public Service Commission (PSC) order. A decision is expected around December. More background is in our October 26 sweep.
