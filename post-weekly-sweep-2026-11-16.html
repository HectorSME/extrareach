ExtraReach is still in the planning stage. This week’s sweep (roughly November 9–16) is a quiet one. International Passive House Open Days ran without Virginia, Maryland or DC stops, and we’re correcting last week’s wording on Street Smart in Brooklyn. There is also a contemporary Philadelphia duplex under construction, an awards evening this week, and rebate deadlines still on the calendar.

Open Days ran with no Mid-Atlantic stops

International Passive House Open Days ran November 13–15. The Passive House Network (PHN) listings included no Virginia, Maryland or DC stops, and there is no recap yet. The nearest certified contemporary example for a tour is still in New York.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Watch for Mid-Atlantic listings in a future Open Days cycle. For now, the confirmed certified contemporary examples remain in New York.

Sources: Passive House Network Open Days

Correction on Street Smart Brooklyn

Last week’s sweep called Street Smart, at 369 Manhattan Ave in Brooklyn, certified by the Passive House Institute (PHI). That overstated the status. PHN’s Open Days listing describes the project as in the final stages of construction and Passive House Classic Certification. The architect, ZH Architects, describes it as certified, but we have not found a PHI certificate or database entry. Street Smart is nearing certification, not yet certified. The November 9 post has been updated.

475 HQ, at 369 Warren St in Brooklyn, is confirmed, with final Phius (Passive House Institute US) ZERO REVIVE 2021 certification dated February 10, 2026. REVIVE is Phius’s pilot certification program for retrofits. 475 HQ is an office retrofit, not a home.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Treat Street Smart as in progress. 475 HQ is the confirmed certified Open Days example from last week’s list.

Sources: November 9 sweep · Passive House Network Open Days · ZH Architects: Street Smart Brooklyn · Phius certified project: 369 Warren Street

Passive George Philadelphia duplex pursuing Phius certification

Passive George is a 3,200 sq ft contemporary duplex at 1930–31 W. George St in Philadelphia, designed by Bright Common for Solo Real Estate. It is under construction and pursuing Phius certification, with a Phius verifier on the team. It is not certified. It is useful as a style and detailing reference, not as a certified precedent.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

A Mid-Atlantic contemporary reference for form and detailing, not a certified example to cite.

Sources: Hidden City: Deep Green Dwelling, Passive George

Groundbreaker Awards Nov 19 in Philadelphia

Green Building United’s Groundbreaker Awards are November 19, from 6–9 PM, at Cira Centre in Philadelphia. The nominees include Kirkwood Street Passive House in Dover, Delaware, described as Delaware’s first Phius-certified homes, and Northern Liberties Passive Rowhouse in Philadelphia, which holds Phius+ 2018 certification.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

A nearby event this week, with two certified projects among the nominees.

Sources: Green Building United: 2026 Groundbreaker Awards nominees

Pepco rebate reservations due Dec 31

Pepco’s Home Performance with ENERGY STAR reservations and completed applications are due December 31. After that date, new applications wait on the Maryland Public Service Commission (PSC) decision for the 2027 cycle of EmPOWER Maryland, the state’s utility-funded efficiency program.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Mark December 31 if a Pepco-service address is in play for a Home Performance rebate.

Sources: Pepco Home Performance with ENERGY STAR

BGE heat pump water heater rebate ends Dec 31

Baltimore Gas and Electric (BGE) is still running its mail-in heat pump water heater rebate of up to $1,600. Buy the equipment and apply by December 31.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

Another December 31 deadline for BGE-territory projects choosing a heat pump water heater.

Sources: BGE heat pump water heater rebate

DCSEU income-qualified fund early bird Dec 18

The DC Sustainable Energy Utility (DCSEU) Income Qualified Efficiency Fund has an information session on November 17 at noon Eastern Time. Early-bird applications are due December 18, and the final deadline is January 15. More background is in our November 2 sweep.

Takeaway for ExtraReach

The early-bird deadline is December 18 for income-qualified multifamily and community buildings.

Sources: DCSEU Income Qualified Efficiency Fund

Still watching

In DC, Emergency Act A26-0457 remains in effect through January 27, 2027. Temporary Act A26-0430 is under congressional review; a Council report dated November 10 projects it becomes law on December 19, 2026. Final net-zero rules are still due by December 31, 2027. The DC Department of Buildings 2027 construction-code comment period is still listed as “Fall 2026” and has not opened.

New York’s all-electric rule remains paused. No U.S. Supreme Court petition has been filed yet, and the deadline is November 24. Source: Climate Case Chart.

EmPOWER Maryland’s 2027–2029 cycle (Maryland Public Service Commission Case 9890) still has no order. The Commission has administrative meetings on November 18 and November 24; we have not confirmed whether the case is on those agendas. Montgomery County’s all-electric standards are still due December 31.
